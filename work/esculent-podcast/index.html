Esculent: A Sticky History of Mezcal

Lead podcast editor for season 7 of Esculent, a bilingual podcast on the history of mezcal.

I was the lead podcast editor for season 7 of Esculent, a podcast from the FSU Native American and Indigenous Studies Center exploring the sticky history of mezcal.

The season was mostly in English, with some episodes in Spanish, including a dubbed episode that brought a Spanish-language interview to an English-speaking audience.

I edited the episodes and their scripts and mastered the audio for release.

My Role

As lead editor I cut every episode from the raw recordings, tightened the scripts for pacing and clarity, and mastered the final mixes. I also produced the season's dubbed episode, adapting a Spanish-language interview for an English-speaking audience.
