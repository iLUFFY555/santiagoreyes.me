HiGSA Talks is the Hispanic Graduate Student Association's conference, bringing graduate students together for talks, discussion, and community. I photographed the 2026 edition on April 11 at the Student Senate Chambers in the FSU Student Union, covering speakers, sessions, and candid moments throughout the day.

From keynote Q&As to conversations over lunch, the set captures a community sharing ideas and building connections.

My Role

As Photographer, I covered the conference throughout the day: speakers on stage, audience engagement during sessions, and candid portraits between talks.
