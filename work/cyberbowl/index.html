CyberBowl was a Florida State University campus-wide campaign run in collaboration with other universities to raise cybersecurity awareness.

My Role

I designed the campaign's banners, digital assets, and themed visuals. Leaning on a college football aesthetic, I turned cybersecurity topics into graphics that felt familiar to students and drove them to engage with safe online practices. The graphics were used online, shown on campus displays, and printed.
