Chocolates Gogga

Logo and promotional materials for Chocolates Gogga, a local chocolate business in Neiva, Colombia.

  • Marketing & Design
Organization
Chocolates Gogga, Neiva, Colombia
Year
2023
Role
Graphic Designer
Deliverables

  • Promotional Materials

I developed promotional materials and the logo for Chocolates Gogga, a local chocolate business in Neiva, Colombia.

The designs highlighted the brand's artisanal identity and local roots, using rich visuals and warm tones to appeal to regional and tourist audiences.

My Role

I designed the Chocolates Gogga logo and created its promotional materials, including a flyer and a tri-fold brochure. The designs lean on the brand's artisanal identity and Colombian roots, with rich visuals and warm tones aimed at regional and tourist audiences.
