Conservation Pathways

A social media campaign for Conservation Pathways, a Tallahassee-based nonprofit mentoring students in natural resource and environmental fields: a weekly intern showcase reels series connecting community donations to real student stories, built to grow visibility and fund student fieldwork.

Conservation Pathways is a Tallahassee-based nonprofit that mentors students, offers field research, and provides career exploration opportunities in natural resource and environmental fields. But the community barely knew they existed: their Givebutter fundraising page, started in November 2024, had raised $610 by September 2025.

I helped build the social media campaign to close that gap. The plan centers on a weekly "Conservation Pathways Intern Showcase" reels series that ties every donation to real student stories, aiming for 20 individual community donations by the end of Q1 2026 and $1,000 raised between December 2025 and March 2026.

My Role

As Social Media Strategist, I helped develop the campaign from research through rollout plan: diagnosing the donation and awareness gap, shaping the messaging around real intern stories, and building the content and evaluation plan behind the weekly Intern Showcase reels series.
