El Rincón Latino

Part of the marketing team behind El Rincón Latino's marketing plan: focus group research and a full campaign built around authentic Colombian comfort food that feels like home.

For El Rincón Latino, a Colombian restaurant in Tallahassee, I was part of the marketing team behind a full marketing overview and campaign. We started with developmental research, including focus groups with university students, to understand how they choose restaurants and why El Rincón Latino wasn't always their first choice.

The findings shaped a campaign around one idea: authentic Colombian comfort food that feels like home, delivered in a student-friendly and approachable way. The plan targeted Hispanic undergraduates and young professionals around three goals, clarity on what to order, humanization of the brand, and student-friendly value, brought to life through tactics like educational "Never Had Colombian Food?" Reels, a "Lunch + Lunch" promo, "Meet the Fam" behind-the-scenes content, and a "Best Seller" series.

My Role

As Account Planner, I was part of the marketing team behind this campaign. I helped design and run the focus group research, turned the findings into audience insights and the brand proposition, and built the marketing plan and campaign the restaurant could put into action.
