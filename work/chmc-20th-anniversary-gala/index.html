CHMC 20th Anniversary Gala

Led the full marketing plan for the CHMC 20th Anniversary Gala, from social media strategy to event design, raising $18,000 for scholarships.

The 20th Anniversary Gala celebrated two decades of the Center for Hispanic Marketing Communication, bringing together alumni, industry leaders, and honorees for an evening of recognition and community. The event's proceeds fund educational resources and opportunities for CHMC students at Florida State University, supporting the next generation of marketing and communications leaders.

My Role

Leading a team, I owned the gala's marketing end to end: social media strategy, campaign design from the save-the-date through the night-of program, and event communications. I supported logistics, planning, and execution, wrote the script and run of show, and handled VIP and VVIP client services, including itineraries and on-site logistics. I also helped plan and execute linked events, including the Empowering Hispanics in Entertainment panel held earlier that day.
